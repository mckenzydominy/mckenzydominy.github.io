01 — About

Hello,
I'm McKenzy.

A cosy Welsh creator who loves to tell stories.

The person

I'm interested in people. Why we behave the way we do. The worlds we build. The things we believe. The things we pretend not to believe.

I'm a Welsh author, creator and storyteller currently figuring out what happens when you enter a completely new chapter of your life.

I love stories in pretty much every form. Books, films, television, photographs, conversations, overheard sentences, the strange little moments that you don't realise matter until years later.

I'm fascinated by society and the human mind, and I could probably talk about either for several hours if you let me.

This website is where I collect some of those things: the stories I've written, the things I'm making, the places I'm going and the life I'm currently living.

Writing

I write when
I have questions.

I've always written because there was something I couldn't quite figure out.

Sometimes that means asking a question through fiction. Sometimes it means writing because I've reached an answer that feels important enough to share.

I write across genres, but underneath all of them I'm really interested in the same thing: people.

My stories tend to explore human experience, psychology, society, identity and the strange pressures that shape the people we become.

"The genre changes. The question usually doesn't."

The current chapter

Becoming
someone new.

I'm currently documenting what it feels like to step into a new world — university, creativity, friendships, work, independence and everything that comes with growing up.
