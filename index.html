01 — About

I'm figuring
it out as
I go.

I make things because I'm curious about people.

I'm a Welsh author, creator and storyteller interested in the strange, complicated and occasionally ridiculous business of being human.

I could talk for hours about the world, society, psychology, stories and why people do the things they do. Writing has always been one of the ways I make sense of it.

These days, I'm taking that same curiosity into my everyday life — documenting university, friendships, creativity, growing up and whatever happens next.

03 — The Books

Stories I've
left behind.

2021

Why The Good Die Young

A sixteen-year-old's imagining of the afterlife, exploring grief through a young adult fantasy story. My first novel, and a piece of art formed from grief.

2023

Blood Brides

Vampires, young women, capitalism and the patriarchy. My first attempt at bringing together my fascination with literature and sociology.

2024

Cordelia Kills

A Regency story about familial pressure, social expectations and the burden of becoming the legacy of a family that is no longer there.

2025

Starring Leopold Rigby

A turning point. A more mature exploration of human experience, identity and the things that follow us into adulthood.
